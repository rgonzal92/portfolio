# Portfolio

A personal portfolio site: a home page that showcases projects and a printable resume.

## Language

**Featured project**:
The one project the author picks to show full-width ahead of the others. An editorial choice, not necessarily the newest.
_Avoid_: latest project, hero project, top project

**Drawer**:
A panel that slides over the page: the mobile menu or the contact panel. At most one is open at a time.
_Avoid_: overlay, modal, sheet
